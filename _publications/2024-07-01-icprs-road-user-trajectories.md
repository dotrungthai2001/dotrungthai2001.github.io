Work carried out during a research internship at Cerema Occitanie, supervised by Guillaume Saint-Pierre and Pascal Salmane.

We analyse road-user behaviour from aerial-view and smart-camera data using object detection and trajectory extraction, and use the resulting trajectories to build a computer-vision pipeline for road-safety risk analysis.

**Authors:** J. M. Rico Velázquez, P. H. Salmane, G. Saint-Pierre, L. Khoudour, T.-T. Do, E. Martin
